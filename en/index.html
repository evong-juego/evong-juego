Assessment in action

An NGO to set up, three profiles and plenty of decisions

An educational simulation game about assessment. Take part in setting up and running the NGO “InterAcción” from the profile you choose, and carry out its tasks with sound criteria.

Play now Other ways to play
  • 3 profiles, about 20 minutes each
  • English and Spanish

What it is

EVONG. Assessment in Action is a simulation game for educational purposes in which you take on assessment tasks. It is a first-person 2D graphic adventure: the player never appears on screen.

The simulation is set in the creation and management of an NGO called “InterAcción”. It is played from three profiles; each one is like a little game, with different activities within the same setting.

Educational objectives

  • Apply what has been learnt about assessment, setting criteria and making judgements suited to what is being assessed.
  • Make reasoned and consistent decisions, knowing the available options and the goals to be reached, and proposing strategies aimed at achieving them.
Who it is for
University students of any field in their first years. It can also be used at other levels of education and in non-formal and informal settings.
Length
About 20 minutes per profile.
Languages
English and Spanish.
Success index of area

The main variable of the game: it shows whether the assessment tasks of each profile are carried out correctly.

Three profiles, three little games

You start by choosing a profile. Each one has three tasks of its own, and every task asks you to set criteria, judge and decide.

Finance and Quality Coordinator

The NGO’s finances, cost control and making sure the organisation meets its quality criteria.

  1. Setting budgetsGet quotes for office supplies and cleaning from three companies and choose the most suitable one.
  2. Controlling costsAllocate the funds according to the budget and the NGO’s financial guidelines.
  3. Institutional self-appraisalWith the NGO’s report, in a session at the Quality Training Centre.

Project Coordinator

Design and delivery of the NGO’s cooperation projects.

  1. Reviewing and submitting the projectCheck that it meets the requirements of the call for funding before sending it.
  2. Selecting applicants for a new positionAssess three candidates against the job specification.
  3. Consultancy for “Ecological Villages”Help another NGO with the evaluation of its project.

Institutional Relations Coordinator

The NGO’s external relations: corporate image, training and encouraging people to get involved.

  1. Designing the corporate imageChoose, with the designer, the poster that best fits the NGO’s image.
  2. Providing information to volunteersGive a short talk and check that it has been understood.
  3. Gaining new membersAt the Local Business Fair, offer each person the way of collaborating that suits them best.

Presentation videos

The videos are on YouTube and only load from there when you click on them.

How to play

Recommended

In your browser

Nothing to install, on a computer, tablet or phone.

  1. Click Play now and then Play. The game’s ES button switches to Spanish.
  2. On a computer, left-click to look and move around; right-click shows the actions for objects and people. On touch screens, just tap.
  3. Esc, or the ☰ button on tablets and phones, opens the menu to save and load your game.

Play now

Original version

For Java, offline

The 2015 version, exactly as it was released, is preserved in Zenodo.

  1. Install Java 8 or later, for example the free Eclipse Temurin.
  2. Download evon_eng.zip (English, 43 MB) or evong.zip (Spanish, 46 MB) and unzip it.
  3. Double-click the .jar file.
